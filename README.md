## 1. Executive Summary: What This Project Does & Current Ground Truth

This repository implements and benchmarks three competing paradigms for predicting cancer cell-line drug sensitivity ($y_{dc} = \ln(\mathrm{IC}_{50})$) on **Genomics of Drug Sensitivity in Cancer (GDSC2)** and **DepMap/CCLE** data:

1. **Arm A (Data-Driven Chemical Baseline)**: Predicts sensitivity purely from compound chemical structure (1,024-bit hashed SMILES fingerprints) and macroscopic cell-line biology features (TMB, ploidy, MSI status, growth morphology), without target, pathway, or literature priors.
2. **Arm B (Biological Pathway Graph Prior GNN)**: Encodes cell lines and drugs over protein–protein interaction (BioSNAP PPI) and pathway (Reactome) graphs, falsified via Maslov–Sneppen degree-preserving edge rewiring.
3. **Arm C (Biomedical LLM Biological Prior)**: Uses multi-stage LLM-distilled pharmacological mechanism profiles (`qwen/qwen3.8-27b` via Groq API) projected into a 768-D semantic space and fused with cell features via gated bilinear multiplication ($\mathbf{u}_{\text{prior}} \odot \mathbf{v}_{\text{cell}}$).
4. **Tri-Arm Stacking Ensemble**: Combines Arm A, Arm B, and Arm C predictions via a constrained convex blend ($w_A = 0.10, w_B = 0.28, w_C = 0.62$).

### Pipeline Overview & Key Benchmarks
* **Real Screening Data**: `C:\Users\USER\Downloads\bioprior-main\bioprior-main\data\processed\training_table.csv` contains **151,973 real GDSC2 pairs** across **172 unique drugs** and **962 cell lines**. Legacy synthetic GBM files (`dose_response_raw.csv`, `sensitivity_summary.csv`, `drug_annotation.csv`) in the root folder belong to an **abandoned Phase 1 pilot** and are excluded from all active pipelines.
* **Live Groq API Priors**: 762 live completions from `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` are logged in `arm_c_groq_live_audit.json` and vectorized into `arm_c_full_scale_drug_embeddings.npy` ($172 \times 768$). Additionally, `new_arm_c_embeddings.npy` ($172 \times 768$) holds `S-PubMedBert-MS-MARCO` embeddings generated by `arm_c_new_pipeline.py`.
* **Per-Disease Cold-Cell Benchmarking (Arms A, B, C & Ensemble)**: Evaluated across 15 isolated human cancer lineages using strict 85/15 **cold-cell disjoint splits** (12,871 unseen test pairs across the 15 diseases) with 15 unified regression, rank, and sensitivity classification metrics stored in `new results/`.
* **Legacy Pan-Cancer Cold-Drug Track (v3)**: An earlier pan-cancer cold-drug evaluation (595 cell lines, 142 targeted drugs, static 2,000-gene graph, 30-drug lockbox, Maslov–Sneppen rewiring) yielded $\Delta r_{\text{residual}} = +0.0313$, 95% CI $[-0.0001, +0.0646]$ (per-drug $\Delta r \approx +0.0039$), motivating the redesigned per-disease lineage-conditioned Arm B architecture.

## 3. Data Processing Funnel & Feature Engineering

### 3.1 Upstream Curation (`bioprior-main/src/data/`)
1. **Raw Screening Labels**: `GDSC2_fitted_dose_response_27Oct23.xlsx` (~295 drugs, ~242,036 pairs). Target label is `LN_IC50` ($\ln(\mathrm{IC}_{50})$ in $\mu\text{M}$; lower = more sensitive).
2. **Compound Matching & Deduplication (`build_drug_map.py`, `dedupe_drugs.py`)**:
   * Matched GDSC2 drug names to ChEMBL 37 via exact name $\to$ synonym $\to$ fuzzy match ($\ge 90$), yielding 179 matched / 116 unmatched. Four implausible fuzzy matches were removed manually, and duplicate compounds under two IDs were collapsed to canonical IDs (`drug_id_canonical_map.csv`), yielding **172 unique drugs**.
3. **Cell-Line Mapping (`build_cellline_map.py`, `load_model_mapping.py`)**:
   * Joined Sanger Model IDs (`SIDM...`) to Broad/CCLE IDs (`ACH-...`) via `sanger_model_list.csv` $\to$ `cellline_id_map.csv`.
4. **Master Training Table (`build_training_table.py`)**:
   * Inner-joined GDSC2 + `cellline_id_map.csv` + `drug_id_map_deduped.csv` and dropped missing SMILES/labels, producing **`training_table.csv`** (**151,973 rows**, **172 drugs**, **962 cell lines**, **24 pathways**).

### 3.2 Two Divergent Downstream Data Regimes (Important!)
The project split into **two distinct data regimes** depending on the experimental track:

| Dimension | Legacy Arm B Pan-Cancer Track (v3) | Current Per-Disease Cold-Cell Track (Arms A, B, C) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Split Type** | **Cold-Drug** (whole structural families held out: Tanimoto $\ge 0.5$; 112 dev drugs in 5 folds + 30 lockbox drugs) | **Cold-Cell** (85% train cell lines / 15% unseen test cell lines, isolated within each of top 15 cancer diseases) |
| **Cohort Size** | **595 cell lines**, **142 drugs**, **76,291 pairs** (after dropping lines missing any of Expr/Mut/CNV, averaging 6,414 duplicate pairs, and dropping 30 drugs without targets) | **All 962 cell lines**, **all 172 drugs**, **151,973 pairs** across 42 diseases; top 15 diseases hold **12,871 unseen test pairs** |
| **Cell Features** | 2,000 genes $\times$ `[expr z-score, mut 0/1, cnv -1/0/+1]` | Arms A & C: 4-D standardized Sanger features `[log1p(TMB), ploidy, msi_status, growth_properties]`; Arm B Blueprint: 4-channel tensor `[z_expr, mut_flag, cnv_state, cnv_present_flag]` |
| **Graph Edges** | 88,332 BioSNAP PPI + 404 Drug–Gene + 18,678 Reactome Gene–Pathway (977 pathways) | Blueprint: Lineage-gated PPI weighted by expression + PCC over 1,500 target-enriched genes + 50 oncogenic pathways |

### 3.3 Chemical Fingerprints (`smiles_to_fp`)
Across `train_arm_a_per_disease.py`, `train_arm_b_per_disease.py`, and `train_arm_c_downstream_new.py`, molecular fingerprints are computed via `smiles_to_fp(smiles, n_bits=1024)`:
* **Implementation**: Extracts character $k$-grams ($k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$) directly from the SMILES string and sets `fp[md5(sub) % 1024] = 1.0`.
* **Tradeoff**: Zero external RDKit dependency (never fails on Windows/Colab environments), deterministic, and fast. However, SMILES character $k$-grams are sensitive to SMILES canonicalization/tautomer formatting and do not encode true circular Morgan topological neighborhoods as cleanly as RDKit `GetMorganFingerprintAsBitVect`. *(Note: `bioprior-main/src/arm_a_baseline/features.py` uses RDKit when available.)*

### 3.4 Arm C LLM Priors & Dual Embedding Implementations
Two different Arm C embedding matrices exist in the repo:
1. **`new_arm_c_embeddings.npy` ($172 \times 768$)** — generated by `scripts/arm_c_new_pipeline.py`:
   * Calls `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` on Groq with a 3-stage PromptSE prompt (Stage 1 Entity $\to$ Stage 2 Mechanism $\to$ Stage 3 4-sentence Profile) saved to `new_arm_c_drug_profiles.json`.
   * Encodes `stage3_profile` using **`pritamdeka/S-PubMedBert-MS-MARCO`** (`SentenceTransformer`, L2-normalized).
2. **`arm_c_full_scale_drug_embeddings.npy` ($172 \times 768$)** — generated by `scripts/run_full_scale_arm_c_pipeline.py` (used by `train_arm_a_per_disease.py` and `train_arm_b_per_disease.py`):
   * Executes **762 live Groq calls** (`qwen/qwen3.8-27b`, logged in `arm_c_groq_live_audit.json`): 172 drugs $\times$ 3 stages (516) + 15 diseases $\times$ 3 stages (45) + 172 cross-refs + control pairs + retries.
   * **Important Tradeoff**: `run_full_scale_arm_c_pipeline.py` (line 332) encodes the generated text with `sklearn.feature_extraction.text.HashingVectorizer(n_features=768, norm="l2", alternate_sign=True)` rather than `S-PubMedBert-MS-MARCO`. HashingVectorizer is instantaneous, dependency-free, and preserves exact gene/pathway keyword overlap (e.g., *EGFR*, *KRAS*, *BRAF*, *MGMT*), at the cost of losing dense synonym semantics compared to PubMedBERT.

### 3.5 Lineage Selection Rationale (Why These 15 Cancer Diseases)
Rather than pooling all 42 GDSC2 designations together or evaluating tiny rare cohorts, the benchmark isolates the **Top 15 specific named cancer lineages ranked by total screening pair count** in `training_table.csv` (excluding only the heterogeneous catch-all label `"Other Solid Cancers"` at 4,630 pairs / 31 cells, which mixes unrelated tissues):
1. **Statistical Power Under 85/15 Cold-Cell Splits**: Selecting lineages from **Non-Small Cell Lung Carcinoma** (13,780 pairs, 87 cell lines) down to **Ewing's Sarcoma** (3,764 pairs, 23 cell lines) guarantees that holding out 15% of cell lines still provides **3 to 13 completely unseen test cell lines** and **506 to 2,037 test pairs** per disease. Diseases below this cutoff (e.g., $\le 20$ cell lines) produce unstable Pearson $r$ and ROC-AUC estimates on 1–2 holdout cells.
2. **Lineage & Biological Diversity**: The 15 selected cancers span **solid epithelial carcinomas** (NSCLC, SCLC, Breast, Colorectal, Ovarian, Kidney, Pancreatic, Gastric, Esophageal), **CNS malignancies** (Glioblastoma), **neural crest / pediatric sarcomas** (Neuroblastoma, Ewing's Sarcoma), **cutaneous melanoma**, and **hematologic/suspension malignancies** (Acute Myeloid Leukemia, B-Cell Non-Hodgkin's Lymphoma). This directly tests whether biological priors generalize across distinct tissue architectures, driver mutations (*EGFR*, *KRAS*, *BRAF*, *MYCN*, *TP53*), and growth morphologies.

### 3.6 Tri-Arm Ensemble Design & Weight Rationale ($w_A=0.10, w_B=0.28, w_C=0.62$)
1. **Why Constrained Convex Stacking ($\sum w_m = 1, w_m \ge 0$) Instead of a Non-Linear Meta-Learner**:
   * Under per-disease cold-cell splitting, individual holdout folds contain 3–13 unseen cell lines. Training a high-capacity non-linear meta-learner (such as an MLP or tree-based stacker) on top of Arm A, B, and C outputs risks overfitting to validation noise on smaller diseases.
   * Convex linear blending introduces minimal degrees of freedom, guarantees bounded variance across unseen cell lines, and preserves the native $\ln(\mathrm{IC}_{50})$ scale without drift.
2. **Why the $0.10 / 0.28 / 0.62$ Split**:
   * **Arm C ($w_C = 0.62$)**: Carries the primary weight because its 768-D LLM mechanism priors with gated bilinear interaction ($\mathbf{u} \odot \mathbf{v}$) achieve the strongest individual generalization ($r=0.9042$, $\text{MSE}=1.5391$), resolving drug–biomarker and synthetic lethal relationships.
   * **Arm B ($w_B = 0.28$)**: Provides substantial complementary weight by grounding predictions in BioSNAP PPI signaling topology and $K=50$ Reactome pathway pools, capturing multi-omics pathway buffering not explicitly encoded in drug-level text summaries.
   * **Arm A ($w_A = 0.10$)**: Acts as an empirical 2D chemical scaffold regularizer, stabilizing predictions for compounds with sparse pathway links or less-characterized literature priors.
   * **Tradeoff & Payoff**: Blending orthogonal modalities (SMILES substructures + PPI/pathway graphs + biomedical text) reduces macro MSE by **23.2%** vs. Arm C alone (`1.5391` $\to$ `1.1820`) and lifts Pearson $r$ from `0.9042` to **`0.9285`** (`ROC-AUC`: `0.9256` $\to$ **`0.9482`**), at the cost of requiring inference across all three model branches.

## 4. Historical Failure Modes, Design Decisions & Tradeoffs

### Failure Mode 1: Cell ID Embedding Leakage (`r ≈ 0.92` Illusion)
* **Where**: `scripts/train_arm_c_downstream_new.py` (lines 89, 109, 130) and `scripts/run_arm_c_arm_a_per_disease.py`.
* **What happened**: Models used `nn.Embedding(n_cells, 64)` alongside random 85/15 pair splits. Because the same cell lines appeared in train and test, the network acted as a matrix-factorization lookup table (`val_pearson = 0.9161` random split vs. `test_pearson = 0.5444` cold-drug split in `new_arm_c_downstream_metrics.json`).
* **Fix**: Deleted `nn.Embedding(n_cells)` entirely and enforced **strict 85/15 Cold-Cell Disjoint Splits** (`tr_cells ∩ te_cells = ∅`) in `train_arm_a_per_disease.py`.

### Failure Mode 2: Flat Constant Predictions Across Cell Lines
* **What happened**: Removing `nn.Embedding(n_cells)` while feeding only drug SMILES left zero cell-specific input. Every cell line received the exact same prediction for a given drug (e.g., `-1.445` for Camptothecin).
* **Fix**: Injected 4 real biological features per cell line from `sanger_model_list.csv`: $\log(1+\text{TMB})$, `ploidy`, `msi_status` (MSI vs. MSS), and `growth_properties` (Adherent vs. Suspension), standardized per disease. Predictions now vary across cell lines ($\sigma > 0.35$ per drug).
* **Tradeoff**: 4 macroscopic genomic features avoid overfitting on small per-disease cohorts (some diseases only have 3–5 unseen test cell lines), but they lack gene-level mutation/expression resolution (which is why Arm A tops out lower than Arm B/C).

### Failure Mode 3: Global Drug Potency Dominance & Multimodal Conditioning
* **What happened**: Because GDSC2 drug mean $\ln(\mathrm{IC}_{50})$ ranges roughly from $-4$ to $+8$, any model knowing drug identity/SMILES achieves $r \approx 0.88$ globally simply by predicting drug means. Standard concatenation of Arm A and Arm C features converged to nearly identical correlations ($0.8893$ vs. $0.8920$, $\Delta r = +0.0027$).
* **Design Decision & Tradeoff**: Arm A is formulated strictly as an empirical chemical + 4-D cell covariate MLP without target priors, while Arm C replaces flat concatenation with a **Gated Bilinear Multiplicative Interaction ($\mathbf{u}_{\text{prior}} \odot \mathbf{v}_{\text{cell}}$)** followed by GELU fusion layers in `train_arm_a_per_disease.py`. Evaluating within-disease cold-cell partitions with bilinear gating amplifies target–cell conditional sensitivity (raising $\Delta r \ge +0.134$ across all 15 cancer types), trading simple linear additivity for higher-order cross-modal interaction capacity.

### Failure Mode 4: Why Legacy Pan-Cancer Arm B (v3) Missed Significance
* **Outcome**: On the 30-drug lockbox, `B_real - B_shuf_dg` achieved $\Delta r_{\text{residual}} = +0.0313$, 95% CI $[-0.0001, +0.0646]$ (missed significance by `0.0001`), per-drug $\Delta r \approx +0.0039$.
* **4 Root Causes**: (1) Static pan-cancer PPI edges added cross-tissue noise; (2) fixed scalar mixing (45% own, 28% PPI, 27% pathway) oversmoothed sparse ($0.59\%$) driver mutations; (3) strict 4-omics intersection dropped 38.1% of cell lines ($962 \to 595$); (4) 1-hop drug$\to$target attention missed downstream signaling cascades.
* **Fix Designed (`ARM_B_PER_DISEASE_GRAPH_PRIOR_BLUEPRINT.md`)**: Lineage-gated PPI subgraphs, 4-channel tensor with CNV missingness flag (`962` lines retained), GATv2 with residual skip connections, $K=50$ Reactome pathway pools, cascade target-to-pathway cross-attention, and gated bilinear head.
